Our Story

Made With Passion, Served With Heart

Founded in 2012 by culinary visionaries, AURA was born from a simple belief: dining should be an immersive journey rather than a simple meal. We combine ancient slow-cooking secrets with contemporary culinary flair.

Every morning, our kitchen welcomes fresh organic produce harvested from partnered regional farmers, sustainably sourced coastal seafood, and hand-milled spices. From our crackling hearth to your table, our hospitality is attentive yet unobtrusive.

“We don't just plate food; we curate moments where aroma, taste, and connection blend seamlessly.”

Arjun Nair

Executive Chef • Culinary Director
